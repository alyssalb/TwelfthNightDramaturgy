Dramaturgy note

Hello everyone! We're so excited to work with you on Twelfth Night. This is going to be an exciting, high-concept production with a setting in the modern day Florida Keys, which gave us a ton to dig into as a dramaturgy team leading up to rehearsals, and a ton more to talk through and figure out as a cast and creative team during the rehearsal process. Our approach to dramaturgy is hands-on and living/breathing, so you'll see us a lot in the rehearsal room (especially as we get started) and we'll continue to update this website with new information and ideas as they develop in the room. We are also always available for what Alyssa calls "Side Quests", which means if you have specific questions about your character, their circumstances, the world of the play, etc. we are always excited to help talk through these things with you and do additional research to make sure we are all on the same page and super immersed in the world of the play we are building together.

We can't wait to really get into this production with you all! Please reach out at absolutely any time to Alyssa Barrack (alyssa.barrack@ucf.edu) by email with any questions or ideas you may have. I'd love to chat!

Sincerely,
The Twelfth Night Dramaturgy Team (Alyssa, Nora, Ilsealysha, and Sam)
